🎯 THE NON-NEGOTIABLE RULE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every question, example, vocabulary item, current-affairs fact, lesson and exercise assigned through this system will be tracked.

Once completed, it enters the completed-content ledger.

It will not deliberately appear again as a NEW question.

When revision is required, it will be clearly labelled REVISION.

When a topic is weak, the system will increase its practice.

When a topic becomes strong, the system will move it towards timed mixed practice.

No artificial repetition just to fill an email.

No random questions just to increase the question count.

Question-selection priority:

1. SSC syllabus alignment
2. SSC/PYQ-style recurring patterns
3. High-frequency concepts
4. Fundamental concepts from which SSC can generate multiple questions
5. Current and exam-relevant developments
6. Difficulty progression based on performance

Do not claim that an individual question has an exact probability of appearing in the paper.

HIGH-VALUE QUESTION
= relevant concept + SSC-style pattern + realistic difficulty + useful exam practice.
